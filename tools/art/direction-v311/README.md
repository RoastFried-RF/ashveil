# Original ground studies — preliminary map review passed

Nine new64px drawings replace the canonical grass0–2, grassdirt0–2 and dirt0–2 frames. Their world footprint remains16px. All leaf, clump, scuff, grit and rut polygons are authored in `draw-ground.mjs`; Inkscape rasterizes the SVG. Existing image pixels are not edited. Source and artwork are dedicated under CC0 to the extent of contributor rights.

Official Stoneshard campsite and Eslabong combat screenshots were reopened for ground/actor contrast and clustered materials. The source does not trace their geometry, sample their colors or contain their pixels. R1's repetitive thin fork tufts and shared horizontal bands were rejected. The r2 source/PNG was independently regenerated byte exactly and ten1440px game screenshots were opened, covering day/torch,2/vision/3zoom, save restoration and privacy. Final full and two-resolution integration checks remain pending. No full-art completion is claimed.

From the repository root:

```sh
node tools/art/direction-v311/draw-ground.mjs
inkscape tools/art/direction-v311/ground.svg --export-type=png --export-filename=public/sprites/original/direction-v311/ground.png
python3 tools/art/direction-v311/install.py --check
```

`--write` installs only the reviewed frame metadata. It never alters image pixels. The provenance hashes must match the output and SVG before installation. Any revised drawing needs a new explicit review and provenance record.
